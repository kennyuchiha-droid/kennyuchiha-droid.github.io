Privacy policy

hmmmmmail is used only by its owner, on his own Gmail account.

  • It asks Google for read-only access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • It reads only receipts and bank notices from a fixed list of senders.
  • What it reads is processed and stored only on the owner's computers at home. Nothing is shared, sold or sent to any other service.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
